State Park Kayak Launch Ramp → Morro Rock & Back

This classic ~3.5-mile round-trip kayak route runs from the State Park Marina north to Morro Rock, across to the Sandspit dunes, and back south. Easily finished in under 1 hour when the rising tide assists your return south.

Free parking lot • Protected concrete ramp & mudflat edge
Tide Strategy: Turn around at Morro Rock (~38 mins in) when the tide is rising so incoming water pushes you effortlessly south back to the ramp.

Kayak Wildlife & Safety Advisory

Sea Otters & Seals: Keep 50–100 ft from resting sea otters and harbor seals near the marina and Morro Rock.

Boat Traffic: Hug the channel edges and stay clear of commercial fishing boats and harbor tour vessels.

Daylight Only & Morning Glass: Avoid launching after dark or within 45 mins of sunset. Morning offers the calmest water before NW winds pick up past 12:30 PM.
